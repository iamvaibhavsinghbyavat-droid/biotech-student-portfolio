About Me

I'm Vaibhav, a first-year Biotechnology student at JECRC University in Jaipur. I chose biotechnology because I like understanding how living things work, and how that knowledge can be used to solve real problems in health, food and the environment.

Genetics is the topic that excites me most. I find it amazing that a tiny code inside every cell can shape so much about an organism, and I'm enjoying learning how scientists read and study it.

I'm also learning about AI and data, because modern biology depends on them more every year. I'm still at the beginning, but I try to use AI tools carefully, check what they tell me, and keep learning the basics properly. Right now my goal is to build a strong foundation and find chances to learn more.
